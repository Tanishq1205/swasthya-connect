eSanjeevani Teleconsultation

Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY) & MJPJAY

Provides health cover up to ₹5 lakh per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalization in empanelled hospitals.

Basic Preventive Health Tips

  • Wash hands regularly with soap for at least 20 seconds.
  • Drink clean, boiled, or filtered water to prevent water-borne illnesses.
  • Schedule routine blood pressure and sugar screenings at local PHCs.
